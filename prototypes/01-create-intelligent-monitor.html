Controls how much evidence Brain requires before creating an incident or declaring an outage.

Balanced
Conservative Balanced Aggressive
Conservative — Brain requires strong evidence across multiple signals before acting. Best for critical production services.

Balanced — Brain weighs evidence proportionally. A good default for most services.

Aggressive — Brain acts on early signal evidence. Best for services where fast response outweighs the cost of investigation.
