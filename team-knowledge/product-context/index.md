# Brain overview

> [!NOTE]
> Brain is currently in Private Preview, scoped to the [Azure Quality Critical Services (QCS)](https://eng.ms/docs/initiatives/quality-and-security-critical-programs-qei-sfi/cohorts/qcs) list with select exceptions. Public Preview is expected in 2026, at which point we anticipate that all services will be able to configure Brain.

Brain is an AI-powered solution for cloud service engineers to manage service health and handle issues efficiently, improving service quality and reducing toil.

### Benefits of Brain

Using Brain, you can detect and mitigate outages faster than you can otherwise and notify customers sooner using [Auto Comms](https://eng.ms/docs/products/brain/auto-comms/auto-comms-overview). You can automatically triage issues to the responsible service and diagnose root causes by using AI. After you adopt Brain, you can measure and track how Brain is helping improve the health of your service.

Use Brain to:
- Detect outages **68% faster** than without Brain integration.
- Mitigate outages **45% faster** than without Brain.
- Notify customers **26% sooner** than without Brain.
- Prevent over **1,500** customer-impacting deployments monthly.  
*Data based on 5/1/2024–10/31/2024 in [Azure Service Health - Brain Follow up 12 17 2024.docx](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:w:/t/AzureQualityInfrastructure/EdHLkuNdIbFAp3pDdQW8o-IB8NvwMuQOg1S5gkPDLplmjg?e=9RsHqn)*

### Improve customer experience using Brain

Using Brain integration, you can lower the chance of outages having [Critical Situations (CritSits)](https://eng.ms/docs/cloud-ai-platform/azure-cxp/cxp-cre/crisis-management/azure-incident-management/azure-incident-management/process/critsits). A *CritSit* is any Support Request (SR) for a Unified or Premier Support customer that is Severity A/1. Brain helps reduce customer support requests for your service by detecting outages earlier and notifying customers automatically when an outage occurs.

From April through September 2024, Azure QCS outages that Brain declared were **3x less likely** to have CritSits than outages not declared by Brain (13% vs. 38%).
