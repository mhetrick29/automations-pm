### Current State

*Guidance (required):* What is the observable state of the world today — before this work ships? Describe what a customer experiences now, what metrics look like at baseline, and what pain or gap exists. This is the "before" to the "End of [Release] State" below. A reviewer should be able to independently verify the current state without reading the rest of the spec.

`<Text>`

### End of [Release] State

*Guidance (required):* In 1–3 sentences, what is the concrete end state for a customer at the close of this release? What can they now do that they couldn't before? This is NOT the overall vision — it is what this specific release ships. A reviewer should be able to read this and immediately understand the boundary between this release and the longer roadmap.

## Appendix A: Why Not X?

*Guidance:* Address 2–4 alternative approaches the team considered and rejected. State the alternative, why it was tempting, and why it was ruled out. This section prevents relitigating decisions in review and gives reviewers confidence that obvious alternatives were considered. At minimum, address: (1) the most common "why not just do Y instead?" question you expect to receive, and (2) any approach that was seriously discussed but deprioritized.
