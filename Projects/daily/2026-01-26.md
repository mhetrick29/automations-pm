# Intelligent Monitors — Daily Summary _(Jan 26, 2026 · Pacific Time)_

## Today’s Highlights
- **Product direction & timeline.** Intelligent Monitors (IM) unify multiple signals and models under a single customer‑facing monitor, with auto‑tuning from user feedback. Target milestones: **OPM via IM UI by end of March**; **all Brain models usable in IM by end of June**; drive toward IM as the default by fall. - **Customer experience principles.** Emphasis on explainability (clear “why” for outages), preview/what‑if analysis before enabling, and guided configuration in one place. [1](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)[2](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)

Legacy per‑SLI/ per‑model monitors are “model monitors”; **Intelligent Monitor** abstracts these details for customers. ## Meetings

### AIOps Product Talks — *Intelligent Monitors* (9:10–10:00 AM PT)
**Key discussion & decisions**
- **Why IM now:** Per‑SLI/per‑model validation is slow and manual; many monitors stall before outage‑mode. IM streamlines setup, unifies signals/models, and learns from user labeling. [1](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)  
- **How IM works:** Single configuration pane; Brain auto‑selects/weights models by signal characteristics; customers can preview historic performance and run what‑if analyses. [2](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)  
- **AOD relationship:** Turning on Brain detection should equate to turning on outage mode; under the hood, Brain governs which model‑monitors can declare outages based on performance. [2](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)  
- **Roadmap checkpoints:** IM UI first supports OPM **by March**; broaden to all models **by June**; maintain classic views during transition. [2](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADMzN2VjYmJiLWNhNDUtNGFlNi05NDBjLTkyNzZmZGMyNmE4MAFRAAgI3lxt2UvAAEYAAAAAu9LBMF7WaE6iQAqSYO_qigcAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAAAAABDQAAN-HaaUs-wUG0XMYKwtcQGQAE9YKpxwAAEA%3d%3d)[3](https://microsoft-my.sharepoint-df.com/personal/mhetrick_microsoft_com/Documents/Forms/DispForm.aspx?ID=135429&web=1)
